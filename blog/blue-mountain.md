Draft

One beautiful early Fall day, after I'd put new sprockets, chain, and 50/50 knobby tires on my DR, I wanted to keep riding to make sure all was well set up. I didn't really need them, but I  had the Tusk hard cases mounted, with some snacks and drinks, and a few extra tools. I decided to ride up the road to the [Blue Mountain](https://www.onxmaps.com/offroad/trails/us/montana/blue-mountain) Recreation Area (BMRA). I had a fun, easy ride up the gravel road. Everything was feeling good. When I got to the parking lot, I met a friendly guy named Brent with a nicely set up red KLR650. In hindsight, I should've been wiser like Brent and stayed out of the trails. The little bit of info I'd found described BMRA as allowing motorcycles and "ATVs", so I naively assumed I'd see suitable sized trails and have a pretty quick and easy look around at some new terrain.

The area is pretty hilly and wooded, which I learned the hard way was not going to be as easy to navigate as I presumed. I was more excited than cautious, and so I rolled my DR650, with hard luggage, onto Trail ...
