The Unexpected Delight of My DR650

Many others have already acknowledged it: a bike that hasn't seen a redesign since the mid-90s shouldn't be this much fun. But my 2015 Suzuki DR-650 is  the most versatile motorcycle I’ve ever owned. Getting my latest bike, this DR-650, feels like a sort of a full-circle. My very first bike (the first one I bought and owned) was a red 1983 Suzuki GR650 Tempter. There’s a certain Suzuki DNA that has followed me from that '83 twin to this '15 Thumper: Keep it simple, and keep about the joy of the ride.
<!-- truncate -->

## The Perfect Balance

What makes the DR special? It's the sweet spot where simplicity meets capability. While the motorcycle world has rushed toward complex electronics and fuel injection systems, the DR650 has remained true to its roots with a proven air-cooled, single-cylinder design that's remarkably easy to maintain and repair.

At around 366 pounds wet, it's light enough to handle on technical trails yet substantial enough for highway riding. The 644cc engine provides plenty of torque for both on-road touring and off-road exploration without being overwhelming for newer riders.

## Built for Adventure

I've put countless miles on my DR650, from daily commutes to multi-day backcountry adventures. The bike's durability and reliability inspire confidence when you're miles from the nearest town on a remote forest service road. When you're planning routes like the Montana BDR, knowing your bike can handle whatever terrain you encounter is invaluable.

The upright seating position keeps you comfortable on long rides, and the simple carburetor means you can make adjustments yourself without specialized tools or a laptop. This mechanical simplicity becomes a huge advantage when you're maintaining your bike in remote locations.

## The Modification Community

One of the DR650's greatest strengths is the incredible aftermarket support and passionate community of riders. Whether you're looking to improve comfort, increase performance, or enhance reliability, there's a wealth of knowledge and proven modifications available.

For anyone serious about getting the most out of their DR650, I highly recommend checking out [ProCycle's comprehensive DR650 parts and modifications guide](https://procycle.us/suzuki-dr650-parts-accessories-best-modifications#why-choose-the-suzuki-dr650-motorcycle). ProCycle has become the go-to resource for DR650 owners, offering everything from essential reliability upgrades to complete adventure bike setups.

Their guide covers:

- **Critical reliability mods** like the NSU safety wire kit and countershaft seal retainer
- **Performance upgrades** including carburetor jet kits and pumper carb conversions
- **Comfort improvements** such as wider seats and better suspension
- **Adventure touring gear** for long-distance exploration

## A Platform for Growth

What I appreciate most about the DR650 is how it grows with you as a rider. Start with a bone-stock bike and gradually add modifications as your skills and needs evolve. Want to focus on technical single-track? Upgrade the suspension and add protection. Planning long-distance tours? Install a larger fuel tank and luggage systems. The DR650 adapts to your vision.

## Final Thoughts

The Suzuki DR650 may not be the flashiest or most technologically advanced motorcycle on the market, but it delivers something far more valuable: proven reliability, versatile capability, and endless potential for personalization. It's a bike that lets you focus on the adventure rather than worrying about the machine.

Whether you're considering your first dual-sport or looking to add a reliable workhorse to your stable, the DR650 deserves serious consideration. And when you're ready to make it truly yours, the resources and community support are second to none.
